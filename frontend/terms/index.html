What CropCurve does

CropCurve looks at a photo of a leaf, suggests the likely crop and disease, estimates how fast the disease may spread over the next two weeks using a weather forecast, and suggests care steps and, where available, crop protection products registered for that use in India. It is free and needs no account.

Advice only

CropCurve gives general guidance. It is not professional agronomic, legal or safety advice, and it does not replace an expert who can see your field.

  • The diagnosis comes from an image model and can be wrong, especially for photos that are unclear or of crops it was not trained on.
  • Spread forecasts are estimates from a simple model and weather forecasts, which can also be wrong.
  • Before acting, especially on high-value crops or widespread infection, confirm with your local agriculture officer or Krishi Vigyan Kendra.

Using crop protection products

  • We only list products that were registered for that crop and disease in India's CIB&RC register when we prepared our data. Registrations change, so check a product is currently approved for your crop.
  • Doses shown are guidance converted from official figures. The product label and leaflet are the final authority on dose, mixing, protective clothing and the waiting period before harvest.
  • Use products only as the law allows, including the Insecticides Act, 1968, and never use banned products.
  • Store, handle and dispose of chemicals safely, away from children, animals and water sources.
  • We use generic names. We don't sell products, and we don't endorse any brand or company.
