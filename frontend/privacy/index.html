Privacy policy

Last updated 12 October 2026

CropCurve is built to work without knowing who you are. This page explains the little data we do handle, why, and for how long.

In short

  • You don't need an account. We never ask for your name, phone number or email to use CropCurve.
  • Your leaf photo is analysed and then discarded. We never store it.
  • Your location is used only to fetch your weather forecast, and is not stored.
  • We don't use cookies, advertising or tracking, and we never sell or share data for marketing.

Who we are

CropCurve (cropcurve.me) is run by its founding team: Sai Siddarth, Samarth Anil, Sai Gowrav and Sumedh Mulay. In this policy, “we” and “us” mean that team.

For anything about your data, write to contactus@cropcurve.me.

What we handle and why

This is everything CropCurve processes when you use it.

DataWhy we use itHow long we keep it
The leaf photo you uploadTo identify the crop and disease.Only in memory for the few seconds the check takes. It is never saved to disk or kept.
Your location, if you share it or type a placeTo fetch the weather forecast that the spread forecast and spray timing are based on.Sent to our weather provider (see below). Kept in our server's memory for up to 30 minutes, at about 1 km precision, so repeat checks nearby are quick. Never saved.
Your other answers (how much of the crop is affected, weather you set yourself)To run the forecast for that check.Only for that check.
Technical data: IP address, browser type, time of visit, pages requestedNeeded to deliver the website and keep it secure.Recorded automatically in short-lived server logs by our hosting provider.
Emails you send usTo reply to you.As long as needed to deal with your message, then deleted. You can ask us to delete it sooner.

What we don't do

  • We don't have accounts, logins or user profiles.
  • We don't use cookies, local storage or any other browser storage.
  • We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • We don't sell, rent or share your data for marketing.
  • We don't build profiles of you or make automated decisions about you.

Cookies

CropCurve does not set cookies or store anything in your browser, so there is no cookie banner. If that ever changes, we will update this policy before it happens.

Services we rely on

A few outside services help run CropCurve. Each handles only what it needs:

  • OpenWeather provides the weather forecast. Our server sends it your coordinates or the place name you typed. It does not receive your IP address. OpenWeather privacy policy.
  • Our hosting provider (currently Vercel) runs our servers and keeps the short-lived technical logs described above. Vercel privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts and cdnjs (Cloudflare) deliver the website's fonts and charting library, so they receive your IP address and browser details when a page loads. Google privacy policy, Cloudflare privacy policy.

Sharing your location is always optional. Your browser asks first, and you can decline and type a town or set the weather yourself instead.

Where your data is processed

Our hosting and weather providers may process data on servers outside India. We only use providers that publish their own privacy and security commitments, and we send them no more than each needs.

How we protect it

All connections to CropCurve are encrypted (HTTPS). Photos are never stored, so there is nothing to leak. Access to our servers is limited to the team.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us what personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct or erase it, nominate someone to act for you, and raise a grievance.

Because we keep almost nothing, the only personal data we are likely to hold is an email you sent us. To make a request, write to contactus@cropcurve.me with the subject “Privacy request”. We will reply as soon as we can, and within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

CropCurve is meant for farmers and growers and is not directed at children. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children, and we don't collect identifying data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If we change how CropCurve handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
