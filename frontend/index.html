    How it works

    1. It names the problem

      A neural network trained on about 70,000 leaf photos identifies the crop and the condition, and tells you how sure it is. When it isn’t sure, it shows its second guess.

    2. It reads your weather

      We fetch the next five days of temperature, humidity and rain for your location. Each disease spreads differently, so the same forecast can mean “act now” for one and “wait” for another.

    3. It shows the day that matters

      You see your crop’s health over two weeks with and without treatment, the last good day to spray, and a product matched to the disease.
